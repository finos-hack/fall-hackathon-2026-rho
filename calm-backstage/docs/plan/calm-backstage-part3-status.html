Deviations from the plan

  • D1Plan assumed the cluster could take more load at 3 GiB; it was at 2.57 of 3 GiB. Recreated the calm-backstage profile with 6 GiB / 4 CPUs (k8s/up.sh); only this profile was touched applied
  • D2Flux without its notification controller retried every event 5 times (~15 s per reconcile). k8s/flux-install.cjs removes --events-addr from the two kept controllers applied
  • D3Playwright's ffmpeg has no concat, and there is no system ffmpeg: the retake is one take with a contents card, chapter cards and logged chapter times applied
  • D13The warm-up upgrade (1.0.0 → 1.2.0) failed: Kubernetes refuses switching a live Deployment to Recreate while rollingUpdate settings remain, and server-side apply cannot remove them (rollingUpdate: null tested, no). Flux reported it and the run failed cleanly at its 300 s wait. Fixed as k8s-deploy 1.2.1 (RollingUpdate, maxSurge 0: the old pod stops first), verified over 1.0.0 in 12 s. 1.2.0 stays published with a note to use 1.2.1 applied
  • D12Plan assumed a full from-scratch run (docker compose down -v). Deleting the Compose volumes was refused (by the auto-mode classifier, rightly): the Hub, registry and Gitea are shared with the other checkout on 3100/7107. Not worked around. Instead: the bundles changed in place went back to their published content and the fixes shipped as new versions (k8s-deploy 1.2.0, nisshi-stream-app 1.0.1); the cluster was rebuilt from scratch with yarn k8s:down/up; yarn hub:seed re-run (idempotent). output/ was left in place applied
  • D11Whole-branch review (fresh reviewer) found: the app page was blank in a browser (Backstage's CSP blocked the starters' inline script; curl had hidden it), failed upgrades could not be retried, never-deployed services were offered upgrades, a race in the shared bundle cache, push-bundles committing to Gitea before refusing, and render discarding uncommitted work. All fixed with tests in 07ab829 (194 tests). Two security findings are recorded for the team, not fixed: the unauthenticated app proxy runs app code on the backend's origin, and anyone who can push to Gitea on port 3300 can apply anything through Flux (cluster-admin) applied
